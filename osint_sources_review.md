## Sources

- [x] **1. Alma Research Center — IDF Ground Maneuver in the Human-Shield Area: Digitization, Intelligence and Firepower**
  [https://israel-alma.org/the-idfs-ground-maneuver-in-the-human-shield-area-in-lebanon-digitization-intelligence-and-firepower/](https://israel-alma.org/the-idfs-ground-maneuver-in-the-human-shield-area-in-lebanon-digitization-intelligence-and-firepower/)
  Analyzes the 2024 ground operation — Torch 750 battle management, decentralized intelligence down to battalion level, combined-arms integration. Directly relevant to battalion/brigade commanders. Alma is an Israeli research center specializing in the northern front with access to IDF doctrine.

- [x] **2. Alma Research Center — Key Points of Hezbollah's Military Status (January 2026 Assessment)**
  [https://israel-alma.org/key-points-of-hezbollahs-current-military-status-january-2026-situation-assessment/](https://israel-alma.org/key-points-of-hezbollahs-current-military-status-january-2026-situation-assessment/)
  Current force-structure snapshot: ~25,000 rockets, ~1,000 suicide drones, 40,000–50,000 active fighters, Radwan Unit at ~5,000 (3,000 fighters + 2,000 support). Covers geographic deployment, command degradation after leadership eliminations, Iranian resupply routes. Essential enemy threat picture.

- [x] **3. Alma Research Center — Special Report: One Year Since the Ceasefire (November 2025)**
  [https://israel-alma.org/special-report-one-year-since-the-ceasefire-in-lebanon-the-israeli-thwarting-effort-against-hezbollahs-reconstruction-effort/](https://israel-alma.org/special-report-one-year-since-the-ceasefire-in-lebanon-the-israeli-thwarting-effort-against-hezbollahs-reconstruction-effort/)
  Post-ceasefire reconstruction analysis: Hezbollah's civilian-military fusion (operatives registered as engineers, educators, municipal clerks), specific active areas (Aitaroun, Ayta ash-Shaab), 669 IDF airstrikes broken down by sector. Most current operational picture available.

- [x] **4. INSS — The IDF Ground Operation in Lebanon: Goals, Alternatives and Consequences**
  [https://www.inss.org.il/publication/ground-maneuver/](https://www.inss.org.il/publication/ground-maneuver/)
  Institute for National Security Studies (Tel Aviv University-affiliated) — premier Israeli national-security think tank. Analyzes the decision-making behind the October 2024 ground operation: strategic goals, the 98th/36th/146th Division deployments, and definitions of success and failure. Good for operational context.

- [x] **5. JISS — The IDF's Learning Curve Following October 7**
  [https://jiss.org.il/en/lappin-the-idfs-learning-curve-following-october-7/](https://jiss.org.il/en/lappin-the-idfs-learning-curve-following-october-7/)
  Jerusalem Institute for Strategy and Security. Covers the doctrinal shift from deterrence to pre-emption, Torch X (Digital Ground Army) integration down to company level, $1.5B accelerated procurement, FPV drones at platoon level, and simultaneous above/below-ground assault tactics. Directly relevant to commanders.

- [ ] **6. The Washington Institute — The Red-Hot Blue Line**
  [https://www.washingtoninstitute.org/policy-analysis/red-hot-blue-line](https://www.washingtoninstitute.org/policy-analysis/red-hot-blue-line)
  Washington Institute for Near East Policy — widely respected US think tank. Covers Hezbollah's post-Oct 7 attack patterns: 670 attacks in three months, Radwan Unit border-breach exercises, integrated fires (ATGMs + drones + rockets), and the "Green Without Borders" fake NGO providing civilian cover for observation posts.

- [ ] **7. Long War Journal — IDF Locates Hezbollah Tunnel and Weapons (October 2024)**
  [https://www.longwarjournal.org/archives/2024/10/idf-locates-hezbollah-tunnel-and-weapons-as-lebanon-operation-expands.php](https://www.longwarjournal.org/archives/2024/10/idf-locates-hezbollah-tunnel-and-weapons-as-lebanon-operation-expands.php)
  Granular, sourced reporting on what IDF ground forces physically found: Kornet ATGMs, AT-3 Sagger missiles, tunnel shafts, loaded launchers concealed in civilian compounds. Also covers the October 2nd Golani ambush in Odaisseh — ATGM fire from surrounding buildings. Long War Journal is a project of FDD, known for factual operational reporting.

- [ ] **8. Breaking Defense — Israeli Operations in Lebanon Should Draw on Lessons from the Past (October 2024)**
  [https://breakingdefense.com/2024/10/israeli-operations-in-lebanon-should-draw-on-lessons-from-the-past/](https://breakingdefense.com/2024/10/israeli-operations-in-lebanon-should-draw-on-lessons-from-the-past/)
  Respected defense-industry publication. Written at the start of the ground operation — draws tactical warnings from 1982 and 2006: tunnel-heavy terrain, village-based ambush environments (hills + streams), and the command dilemma when Hezbollah withdraws north of the Litani. Relevant for understanding operational constraints.

- [ ] **9. Meir Amit Intelligence and Terrorism Information Center — "Spotlight on Terrorism: Hezbollah and Lebanon" (weekly series)**
  [https://www.terrorism-info.org.il/en/](https://www.terrorism-info.org.il/en/)
  IDC Herzliya-affiliated institute. Publishes weekly Hebrew + English open-source intelligence summaries: specific incidents, operatives eliminated, weapons used, IDF responses. Useful as ongoing situational awareness with primary-source citations. Particularly relevant issues: Dec 2024 – present.

- [ ] **10. US Army Press — "Back to Basics: A Study of the Second Lebanon War"**
  [https://www.armyupress.army.mil/Portals/7/Primer-on-Urban-Operation/Documents/Back-to-Basics.pdf](https://www.armyupress.army.mil/Portals/7/Primer-on-Urban-Operation/Documents/Back-to-Basics.pdf)
  Official US Army study of the 2006 war. Covers Hezbollah's tactical strengths (prepared fighting positions, ATGMs, cover/concealment, IED belts), weaknesses (maneuver warfare, marksmanship, air defense), tunnel/bunker architecture, and IDF failures that led to heavier-than-expected losses. Though based on 2006, Hezbollah's core methods remain consistent. Very high credibility — official US Army publication.
